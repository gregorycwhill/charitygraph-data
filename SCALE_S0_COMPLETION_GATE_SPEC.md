# Scale S0 completion gate specification

**Status:** future gate design; no S0 execution is authorised.

An S0 completion packet must bind the immutable mandate/population/policy/task hashes and show, by subject and claim family: source attempt and rights outcomes; representation mode including visual-PDF disposition; logical versus physical task counts; route/escalation decisions; reservation, actual and unknown-cost reconciliation; durable attempt and restart history; candidate/review/promotion lineage; explicit coverage/missingness; and public-release status.

Acceptance is not zero ordinary semantic corrections. A reviewed correction is acceptable when its scope, evidence, lineage and supersession are preserved. The gate fails on a provider ambiguity without required halt, duplicate transmission, raw-output/card or candidate/governed bypass, rights breach, policy/hash drift, systemic identity/scope defect, repeated same-family defect beyond the mandate threshold, unresolved mandatory-review backlog, false current-state claim, or incorrect promotion.

Before S1 is considered, the product owner must accept the packet's source-family success/failure profile, review workload/dispositions, cost reconciliation, defect analysis, relationship/taxonomy/currentness outcomes and promotion proof. This specification neither selects S1 nor authorises it.
