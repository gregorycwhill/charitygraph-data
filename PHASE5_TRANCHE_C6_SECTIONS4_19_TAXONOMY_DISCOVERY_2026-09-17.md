## Result

§4 is **PARTIAL**. Activities are evidence-bound observations; output, outcome, impact and taxonomy assignments do not become activity facts. §19 is **PARTIAL**. A classification records taxonomy identity/version, concept, status, producer/method and observation lineage. Candidate, accepted, narrowed, rejected, abstained and superseded remain distinct.

`SpecialistInput` adds the smallest v0.2-only atomic projection. It makes source-reported classification and assessed classification distinct predicates. It does not create a taxonomy engine. Local Buying's 50 v0.6 assignments are contaminated because the shared website/domain was treated as target-owned; v0.6.1's 26 survive after evidence and scope are bound to the correct subject. The active adapter rejects the same broadening because subject, `about_subject_ids`, scope, locator, source, time, full payload and lineage must match CardEvidence.

Search scores, embeddings, labels and retrieval metadata may support discovery only. No retained run tests search quality, and no discovery signal becomes evidence without a governed proposition. Native concepts remain evidence-linked and taxonomy-blind; they are neither ground truth nor input leakage.

Residuals: an active assignment-history store beyond the bounded input; independently replayed classifier-blindness at production scale; and empirical search-quality evidence. These do not require acquisition or provider work for this test.
