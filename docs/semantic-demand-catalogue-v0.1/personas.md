# Derived demand personas

These are job-based consolidations of canonical experiences, not demographic or personality archetypes.

| ID | Canonical derivation and job-to-be-done | Decisions / units / output | Evidence sensitivity and semantic failure modes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cohort_analyst` | Experience 4 analyst cohort builder: construct a defensible cohort. | inclusion, comparison; organisation/program/service; filter, table, export. | scope/time mismatch, taxonomy treated as fact, missingness hidden. |
| `service_system_planner` | Experience 5 service-system and competitor map: inspect supply-side service system. | map gaps/referrals; service/site/population/geography; map and bounded inventory. | HQ→delivery geography, description→availability, supply→need. |
| `funder_ecosystem_user` | Experience 6 funding and ecosystem analysis. | diligence, dependencies and flows; organisation/program/funder; graph and evidence brief. | commitment/payment/revenue conflation, double-counted flow, fundability inference. |
| `fundraising_analyst` | Experience 7 fundraising activity analysis. | assess mobilisation evidence; campaign/appeal/reporting entity; timeline/table. | donation revenue→channel proceeds, campaign→audited amount. |
| `finance_comparator` | Experience 9 finance and comparison. | compare reported finances; reporting entity/period/line; comparable table. | incompatible periods/scopes, reported historical spend→next-dollar destination. |
| `evidence_evaluator` | Experience 10 evidence and evaluation review. | inspect result credibility; intervention/population/study; structured evidence review. | output→outcome, association→causation, programme evidence→organisation claim. |
| `mandate_agent` | Experience 12 downstream mandate screening. | screen against user-owned rule; resolved organisation/program; decision packet. | evidence→universal fit, designation→legal restriction, silent→clean. |
| `repro_data_builder` | Experiences 15 research reproducibility and 16 data-builder. | reproduce/query/audit a governed projection; assertion/evidence/release; lineage record/API result. | retrieval time→validity, source authority universalised, missingness collapsed. |
