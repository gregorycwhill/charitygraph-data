## Meaningful-cell demand map

Each domain has at least one `C` or `U` cell.  This compact map states the recurring demand / operation / trap for all meaningful cells, rather than asserting an artificial question quota per cell.

| domain | Primary demand and representative operations | Key trap / non-inference |
|---|---|---|
| 1 identity/regulatory | resolve entity, identifier and reporting scope; lookup/filter | identifier or brand does not prove every activity belongs to it. |
| 2 purpose/cause | filter declared purpose and cause; compare attributed wording | legal purpose is not current activity. |
| 3 programs/services | identify a bounded program/service; trace portfolio | programme scope must not be lifted to organisation scope. |
| 4 activities/SDGs | filter evidenced activity and source-reported classification | taxonomy label does not prove activity. |
| 5 geography/beneficiaries | map population/geography roles; cohort | HQ is not delivery geography; intended is not reached. |
| 6 participation | discover opportunity/role; filter time and eligibility | advertisement is not participation. |
| 7 direct service/capacity | inspect access, hours and availability; exception-find | service description/silence does not prove present availability. |
| 8 fundraising | trace appeal/channel/fundraising activity | promotional claim is not audited channel proceeds. |
| 9 governance/leadership | inspect role/time/group governance | office-holder scope and timing must be retained. |
| 10 people/workforce | compare staff/FTE/volunteer measures | volunteer count is not employee count. |
| 11 scale/capability | inspect qualified capability/constraints | revenue or accreditation does not prove capacity. |
| 12 relationships | trace directed scoped edges/flows | payment/co-occurrence is not durable relationship. |
| 13 finances | compare statement line/period/scope; calculate bounded change | accounting lines, scope and period are not automatically comparable. |
| 14 funding/dependencies | trace funder/award/payment/dependency | award, commitment, payment and revenue differ. |
| 15 ethos/values | inspect attributed commitments and implementation evidence | policy/identity is not implementation. |
| 16 conduct/adverse | inspect formal status, response and correction | allegation/silence does not establish conduct or clean record. |
| 17 notable history | trace dated events/succession | historical fact does not prove current state. |
| 18 evaluation/outcomes | review study/result/limitations | activity/output is not outcome; association is not causation. |
| 19 classification/search | retrieve governed discovery assignment/lens | retrieval relevance is not truth or identity. |
| 20 evidence/coverage/freshness | audit locator, state, review and correction | source silence is not absence; retrieval time is not validity. |

Counts: **C 79, U 65, M 13, N 3**. The retained pilot samples all domains but does not mechanically mirror these counts.
