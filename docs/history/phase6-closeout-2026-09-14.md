## Shared representation carry-forward

The bounded Builder coverage contract added after this review distinguishes positive evidence, `not_found_in_reviewed_sources`, `source_silent`, `not_processed`, `source_unavailable`, `not_acquired`, `processing_failed`, unresolved `unknown` and justified `not_applicable`. Each field binds to an explicit subject, legal scope and named finite reviewed-evidence universe. A not-found state only describes the listed reviewed sources; it is not evidence that the phenomenon or evidence does not exist elsewhere.

Outcomes coverage fields are observed outcome measure, evaluation/assessment, evaluator identity, method, comparator/counterfactual and limitations. Commitments fields are implementation evidence, independent implementation verification, implementation outcome and affirmative evidence of non-implementation. This is an offline Builder contract and test change, not a new public schema, governed record, promotion or Viewer feature.
