# Paired analyst-task materials — Phase 6 reality slices

Status: prepared, not administered. No task time, correctness score, or analyst judgement is recorded here.

For each selected subject and slice, use the same analyst question once in each condition. Counterbalance condition order across subjects if more than one analyst is available. Do not expose condition labels as quality cues. Do not treat either answer as ground truth without proposition-level adjudication.

## Condition A — retained evidence only

Give the analyst the frozen source inventory and locator access identified in the slice packet. Ask them to answer the approved concrete analyst questions in the corresponding section of `PHASE6_REALITY_SLICE_DESIGNS_2026-09-12.md`. Ask for concise findings with source role, exact locator, scope, source date/as-of, explicit unknowns, and whether the evidence supports only activity, output, outcome, contribution, causation, policy, implementation, description, availability, or capacity.

## Condition B — candidate-assisted

Give the analyst the same frozen sources plus the unmodified candidate output(s) and proposition worksheet for that subject. Ask them to answer the identical questions and record which candidates they accept, reject, narrow, or mark unresolved. Do not reconcile repeats automatically. Preserve each differing output for separate disposition.

## Record for each task

- anonymous task and analyst IDs, condition order, start/end times, and active minutes;
- answer completeness against the approved questions;
- proposition-level correctness after independent adjudication, including unsupported claims and material omissions;
- proportion of statements with resolvable evidence locator, source role, scope, and source-bounded date;
- uncertainty and missingness handling, including source silence versus unavailable source;
- correction burden and reviewer time;
- analyst confidence recorded before adjudication;
- any privacy or representation issue.

Compare paired time and adjudicated answer quality per subject; report the full small-sample results, uncertainty, exclusions, and disagreements. Do not claim the design's usefulness hypothesis or advancement threshold is met until the approved human review is completed. No cohort enlargement is authorized by this worksheet.
