# Candidate Semantic Demand Catalogue — Gate 2 semantic review

Status: **candidate for supervisor/Product Owner review; not canonical main**  
Proposed cardinality: **20 executable demands**, one per active North Star domain. This is intentionally not 74: the legacy scaffold is an experiment coverage scaffold, not a justified semantic cardinality.

## Shared execution contract

Each demand is instantiated with `subject`, `scope`, `time`, and any listed parameters. `scope` may be legal entity, operating unit, group/branch/brand, program, service, project, campaign, appeal, fund, site, population, geography, event/transaction, or reporting period. `time` is the observation/effective/reporting interval; it is never silently “current”. A proposition is adjudicated as `SUPPORTS`, `PARTIAL`, `CONFLICTS`, `NOT_FOUND_IN_REVIEWED_UNIVERSE`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, or `UNRESOLVED_PROCESSING_FAILURE`. `NOT_FOUND_IN_REVIEWED_UNIVERSE` means only that the governed reviewed universe did not support the proposition; it is not real-world absence. Evidence policy is proposition-specific: the expected source role is the role competent to support that proposition, with locator, capture time, scope and temporal basis retained.

## Semantic authorship and constraints

The definitions above are model-authored interpretations of current canonical product text. No script chooses a definition, source role, migration outcome, split, merge or retirement. Mechanical validation may check IDs, required fields, legacy coverage and deterministic formatting only. The catalogue does not contain charity-specific answers, rankings, recommendations or fundable-universe truth tables.
