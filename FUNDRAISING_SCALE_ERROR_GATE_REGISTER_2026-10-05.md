# Fundraising scale and error gate register — 5 October 2026

**Status:** Active bounded backlog and gate register.  
**Scope:** Converts the completed 289-era 24-case local regression and the 4 October bounded research gate into repeatable promotion decisions. It creates no acquisition, production ingestion, public contract, release, Viewer, or population-scale authority.  
**Supersession relationship:** This implements the measurement and error-reporting work left by `FUNDRAISING_SUCCESSOR_MATRIX_2026-10-04.md`; the semantic boundary remains governed by `FUNDRAISING_VOCABULARY_MEASUREMENT_RESEARCH_GATE_2026-10-04.md`. Neither is reopened or weakened here.

## 1. Decision rule

`PASS` means a capability may be used only at the named maturity level. `HARDEN` means the local contract is useful but its evidence/error gate is incomplete. `REVIEW_ONLY` means it may be exercised only in a retained, adjudicated cohort. `QUARANTINED` means no downstream consumer may promote it. `BLOCKED` means a separate approval or cross-product decision is required.

No row can pass merely because another row passes. In particular, a direct practice observation does not supply campaign identity, provider identity, currentness, setting, regular giving, money attribution, or finance compatibility.

## 2. Baseline denominator and error ledger

The only completed regression denominator is **24 fixture cases**, not 24 charities, source pages, independent identities, or a representative sample. The cases overlap across assertions; execution counts are not unique cases.

| Baseline outcome | Numerator / denominator | Interpretation | Error result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Practice observations | 12 / 24 | Retained positive practice observations | No independently adjudicated precision/recall rate exists. |
| Confirmed PFRA bindings | 3 / 5 PFRA-binding cases | Binding is retained only where confirmed | Membership is never practice/currentness evidence. |
| Unresolved or ambiguous bindings | 4 / 24 | Refused promotion | Correct fail-closed handling; false-negative rate is not measured. |
| Available money observations | 14 / 24 | Source-native money is present at its stated grain | This is availability, not method/campaign attribution coverage. |
| Available share/bounds observations | 0 / 24 | No real share/bounds case was demonstrated | Bounds and share lanes cannot scale. |
| Finance-without-method-attribution controls | 9 / 24 | Aggregate money remains unattributed | This is a required negative-control class, not a success rate. |

Known false-positive controls include generic purpose/donation copy, PFRA membership, legal permission, historical material treated as current, logo walls/directories, scope leakage, campaign/practice conflation, aggregate-money proximity, and incompatible financial periods/bases. Known false-negative controls include scoped silence, cessation, ambiguous identity, unprocessed source scope, and deliberately unavailable finance. The baseline confirms refusal behaviour for these classes; it does **not** measure their rates.

## 4. Cohort promotion packet (required before any scale reconsideration)

The next cohort must contain **30–50 independently identity-adjudicated, source-backed cases**. Report both its case denominator and each applicable assertion denominator; never turn overlapping test executions into a unique-case count.

For every capability proposed for promotion, the packet must report: retained positives; adjudicated positives; false positives by error class; false negatives/refusals by error class; edits/rejects; unknown/not-found/not-processed/unavailable outcomes; source-family distribution; subject-binding result; time/status and scope accuracy; and excluded cases with reasons. It must separately include F2F cessation, provider ambiguity, campaign/practice separation, finance-only, scoped silence, incompatible finance, contradiction, and aggregate-money non-attribution controls.

No numeric precision threshold is invented here: the approved gate requires that precision dominate recall and that no aggregate score conceal a failure class. A future threshold is a product-owner decision only if the completed packet exposes a genuine trade-off; until then, any unmeasured error class fails closed.

## 5. Non-negotiable quarantines

Do not infer practice from purpose, donations revenue, generic fundraising copy, legal permission, peer priors, or PFRA membership alone. Do not infer agency, setting, compensation, regular giving, organisation-wide scope, continuity, or currentness beyond the source evidence. Do not allocate organisation/portfolio money by proximity or weights. Use `unknown`, `not_found_in_scoped_source`, `not_processed`, `unavailable`, or `null` rather than manufacture coverage.
