| Latest authorised annual report | Conditional/applicable and preferred for financial/reporting-period evidence. | Full document is never sent by default. |
| Wikipedia/Wikimedia | Excluded from S0 baseline; may be a separately authorised context source. | Inclusion requires source-role and rights decision. |
| Fundraising registry/PFRA | Conditional only where jurisdiction and registry role are explicit. | It is not finance or expenditure evidence. |
| Specialist sources | Triggered only for formal regulator/court, accredited register, Indigenous-authority, or claimed relationship resolution. | Each trigger needs a centrally governed source/rights record. |

The narrow default is: local acquisition only when the exact family/artifact has a lawful-access and retention decision; provider transmission only for an exact bound structured record or bounded excerpt. For public websites and PDF prose, use the existing bounded analytical route only if the representation, no-prohibition check and provider-policy record pass. Visual pages are transmitted only as selected rendered pages. Unknown terms, full/near-full documents, ambiguous rights, paywalls, authentication or explicit prohibitions fail closed as `PRODUCT_OWNER_OR_LEGAL_POLICY_REQUIRED`.

| Claim family | S0 rendering default |

| Official purpose/program/activity | Observation-as-of source fact; no implied current availability. |
| Governance/workforce, campaigns, affiliations, commitments/policies | Current-state unknown unless a dated current observation passes a future freshness policy and review. |
| Service availability / capacity | Current-state unknown by default; historical availability remains historical. |
| Conduct, outcomes, dependency, relationships, taxonomy | Dated governed candidate/review outcome only; no universal currentness claim. |

## Routing, bundling and review

| Active family | Candidate route | Evidence and escalation / review rule |
|---|---|---|
| identity/regulator; finance source-native; discovery signals | deterministic | Exact structured fields only; deterministic validation; discovery never becomes evidence. |
| purpose, program, activity/SDG, population/geography, participation, direct service, fundraising, governance, workforce, capability, ethos/commitments, history | Luna primary, Terra escalation | Escalate only for source conflict, scope ambiguity, repeated schema failure, taxonomy narrow/reject boundary, unresolved relationship endpoint, or outcome causation ambiguity; sampled review plus triggered mandatory review. |
| relationships; funding/dependency; outcomes/evaluation | Terra primary or Luna+Terra where the frozen task contract permits | Mandatory review for complex endpoint, dependency, causation, conflict or stale-current-state claim. |
| conduct/adverse | human decision | Any positive, absence conclusion, or ambiguity is mandatory review; no automated substantive promotion. |
| taxonomy | Luna primary + Terra escalation | Taxonomy boundary/disagreement is mandatory review; source wording is not classification authority. |

Candidate physical packet groups: (1) regulator identity + structured finance; (2) first-party purpose/program/activity/population/participation/direct-service; (3) organisation governance/workforce/capability; (4) fundraising + ethos/commitments; (5) relationships alone; (6) funding/dependency alone; (7) outcomes/evaluation alone; (8) conduct alone; (9) taxonomy after semantic candidates. Groups 5–9 remain physically independent because cross-task contamination, stronger routing or mandatory review outweigh reuse. P4-E1 supports compatible bundling, not bundle-by-call-count.

Review defaults: 100% of conduct positives/negatives, causation, dependency, taxonomy boundary, complex relationship, conflict, correction/supersession, stale-current-state and Terra-escalated candidates; 25% deterministic audit; 25% baseline sample for ordinary Luna semantic candidates. With Option B shadow estimates of 160 logical tasks, estimate 24 deterministic outputs, 136 candidate outputs, 44 mandatory reviews and 23 sampled reviews (67 review items); this is a workload estimate, not a rate claim.

### Source-rights calibration amendment

The later `SCALE_S0_SOURCE_RIGHTS_CLEARANCE_2026-09-18.md` adjudicates the
mandate/source boundary. It supersedes only the earlier suggestion that unknown
website/report rights are mandate-blocking external facts. The calibrated plan is
24 `STRUCTURED_BASELINE` deterministic instances, 120 `RIGHTS_GATED` ordinary
semantic instances, 16 `CONDITIONAL_SPECIALIST` relationship/dependency instances,
and 8 `EXCLUDED` human-only conduct instances. It has zero observed physical calls,
reviews or cost. Conditional planning remains 48–120 physical calls, 6–142 unique
reviews and USD 0–8 under the mandate ceiling; no exact call count is restored
before governed source acquisition.

Immediate single-event halt: ambiguous send/billing; any identity/scope systemic error; raw-output-to-card or candidate-to-governed bypass; rights violation; model/prompt/schema hash drift; any budget breach; classification-authority contamination. Count/rate: two same-family schema failures after one permitted repair; two same-family material semantic defects in the first ten reviewed items; review backlog over 25 open mandatory items; any false current-state claim confirmed by review. Warning at 15 open mandatory reviews or one semantic defect; no automatic recovery.
