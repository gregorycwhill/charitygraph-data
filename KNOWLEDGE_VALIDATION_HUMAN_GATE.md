# Knowledge Validation v1 — compact human gate

**Estimated review:** 20–30 minutes. **Decision effect:** validation only; no
candidate becomes public until a later governed publication decision.

## A. Semantic observation adjudication

Open the private Builder packet:
`staging/knowledge-validation-v1/HUMAN_REVIEW_PACKAGE.md`. Review the
48-case deterministic sample, concentrating first on its decision-changing
edge cases: boilerplate (4), rhetoric (5), activity/participation (23),
beneficiary (16), geography (10) and program boundaries (2). For each case
choose: `ACCEPT`, `EDIT`, `REJECT`, `WRONG_DOMAIN`, `INSUFFICIENT`,
`IDENTITY_BLOCKED` or `ADDITIVITY_BLOCKED`; give a rationale except for
`ACCEPT`.

Recommended minimum discussion: adjudicate all 4 boilerplate, all 5 rhetoric,
four activity/participation, four beneficiary, three geography and both
program cases first (22 cases). Then decide whether the remaining stratified
cases need adjudication to establish domain-specific precision.

## B. Taxonomy pressure decisions

Bring the eight named historic private pressure points to the review. For each,
classify it `PERSISTS`, `WEAKENS`, `DISAPPEARS`, `CHANGES` or `NEW` from the
new taxonomy-blind evidence. Decide specifically whether the
activity/participation and program/organisation boundaries are useful enough
to retain. Do not approve a taxonomy change in this packet.

## C. Fundraising semantics

Review five Golden Corpus classes: direct explicit fundraising expenditure;
EJA direct 10% share and mechanical approximately AUD 585,279; ambiguous
marketing/communications/advertising/PR attribution; overlapping/hierarchical
rows (`ADDITIVITY_BLOCKED` unless proven additive); and unavailable measure.
For ambiguous components answer: definite fundraising, possible fundraising,
excluded, source allocation fraction, additive with selected basis, and
whether evidence supports lower/upper/point estimates. Do not calculate or
publish fundraising efficiency or ROI.

## D. Consumer-LLM manual script

Run 8–12 fresh/new/incognito product sessions from
`CONSUMER_LLM_EVALUATION.md`, covering unaided discovery, source discovery,
directed CharityGraph use and supplied-record interpretation. Capture the generic
form there. Treat unaided/source-discovery misses as **INDEXING / DISCOVERY
FAILURE** until pages have had a reasonable indexing opportunity; do not set a
fixed deadline. Directed and supplied-record tests can proceed now.
