## Fixed cohort and method

The fixed retained cohort is Australian Red Cross/Lifeblood, EJA, Fitted for Work, APNIC, World Vision Australia, Tweed and Local Buying. It combines C1–C5 retained records with C6 taxonomy controls, C7 fundraising/commitment controls and C8 relationship controls. Each cell was inspected for traceable positive content or explicit non-negative coverage; representative paths were followed through CardEvidence/relationship, observation, locator, source and lineage.

## Audit result

No source silence, unavailable/unprocessed material, processing failure, unresolved endpoint, missing fundraising or taxonomy material, or historical-only material became a negative assertion. The C1–C8 leakage controls held for unit/program, population/participation, capacity/capability, fundraising/finance/funding, governance/workforce, relationships/proposition ownership, ethos/purpose, outcomes/impact, classification/truth and freshness/current truth.

The original result was **GATE_DRY_RUN_PASS_WITH_BOUNDED_RESIDUALS**. C9 independently red-teamed its assumptions, repaired substantive-content, discovery-epistemic, taxonomy-lifecycle and observation-time defects, and separates representation from empirical, policy and scale residuals. The corresponding proposed real-gate criteria are in [the real completion-gate specification](PHASE5_NORTH_STAR_REAL_COMPLETION_GATE_SPEC_2026-09-17.md). Freshness policy, correction/dispute occurrence and broad relationship-role coverage are not bounded-gate blockers where no unqualified current-state claim is made; they remain policy, empirical or production residuals.
