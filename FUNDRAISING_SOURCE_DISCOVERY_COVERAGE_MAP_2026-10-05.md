These are cases, not charities, pages, independent identities, or a representative source sample.

Accordingly, this map ranks **expected evidentiary usefulness**, not observed prevalence, recall, refresh reliability, legal permission, or production readiness. A surface may be searched without being eligible to promote an observation.

## 3. Ordered discovery policy

For an already identity-resolved charity, search in this order and stop or defer a surface when its source-rights rule, binding requirement or bounded scope cannot be satisfied:

1. Establish the subject and period anchor from regulator/state records and charity identity material. Record the binding basis before interpreting fundraising wording.
2. Look for the most recent available charity-owned annual report and audited statements. Extract source-native finance separately from any practice/campaign observation.
3. Search the charity's own standing fundraising pages using explicit role targets: `Ways to Give`, `Donate`, `Fundraise for Us`, `Regular Giving`, `Gifts in Wills`, `Major Giving`, `Appeals`, and equivalent native labels.
4. Search charity-owned campaign, event, appeal and Giving Day pages. Retain campaign identity, edition and stated dates; do not elevate a campaign to a standing practice.
5. Use regulator/state disclosures to corroborate identity and directly disclosed finance, applying the finance compatibility gate rather than forcing reconciliation.
6. If a direct charity campaign/platform page explicitly identifies the charity, assess it as a bounded review candidate. Do not use platform search results, generic donation widgets or brand-adjacent pages as binding evidence.
7. Only after primary surfaces, use agency/provider, PFRA, award, benchmark and trade material for research discovery or tightly bounded corroboration. These remain research-only under the current gate and must not fill missing practice/currentness/finance fields.
8. Close each source role with a coverage state: `observed`, `not_found_in_scoped_source`, `not_processed`, `unavailable`, `blocked_by_rights`, `ambiguous_binding`, or `incompatible_finance`. Scope includes searched surface, date, terms/rights disposition and any retrieval failure.

This is an order of **evidence quality and refusal safety**, not a mandate to exhaust every public URL. A direct, well-bound primary statement may close the relevant facet; a later secondary source cannot broaden it without its own support.

## 4. Promotion checks at every surface

Before retaining a review observation, require source identity and role; exact wording or extraction locator; charity binding basis; observed and valid time where available; scope/geography/setting where stated; production method; review status; and coverage/missingness. Preserve the native term alongside any candidate canonical facet.

The following do not promote a fact: generic organisational purpose or donation copy; donations revenue; legal permission; PFRA membership alone; peer practice; a provider logo wall; a directory listing; a search snippet; a missing page; or a financial amount adjacent to a method name. Finance follows direct disclosure, deterministic reconstruction, defensible bounds/governed interpretation, then unavailable/null — never proximity allocation.

Contradiction, cessation, ambiguous identity, scoped silence, incompatible finance and unprocessed/blocked-source cases are required cohort classes, not cleanup exceptions. Report their assertion denominators separately from positive observations; do not convert the 24-case regression or overlapping source-role counts into prevalence or precision claims.
