## Semantic and scope rules

- **Outcomes:** activity is what was done; output/reach is a delivered count/product; outcome is an observed change with a stated measure/population/time; contribution is an attributed role; causation requires causal evidence and cannot be inferred from source attribution. Do not upgrade among these.
- **Commitments:** stated aspiration, policy, voluntary pledge, and legal/contractual commitment are distinct from evidence of implementation. First-party implementation claims are not independent verification, observed practice, compliance, or outcome.
- **Capacity/access:** service description or existence does not establish present availability, spare capacity, access eligibility, wait time, or service reach. Unknown remains unknown unless dated, scoped evidence supports more.
- **Scopes:** do not propagate an organisation-wide statement to a program/service or vice versa. RFDS Queensland Section is not the national RFDS. A program or service requires its own scope identity and evidence.
- **Evidence roles:** source fact, first-party claim, independent observation, model candidate, human adjudication, and governed proposition are separate. Model output is never itself a human decision.
- **Evidence universe:** positive and negative states are bounded by an enumerated finite reviewed universe. Silence in one representation does not establish absence elsewhere.

3. **No retroactive rewrite:** the completed source-only answer and task files are immutable under the model-assisted lock. Preserve any later human projection evaluation separately.

5. Review every material answer and every explicit not-found/missingness state. Capture disagreements and rationale; do not resolve disagreement by majority or model vote without explicit human decision.

## Product-value rubric and decision frame

Score each material answer ordinally: **PASS** = correct, materially complete for the reviewed universe, traceable and qualified; **PASS_WITH_MINOR** = substantively correct and traceable with a non-material wording/omission weakness; **FAIL** = materially incomplete, misleading, untraceable, wrongly scoped or less useful than required without a critical epistemic upgrade; **CRITICAL** = untraceable or materially overstated claim, including unsupported outcome/causation, scope collapse, false evidence-of-absence, false comparability, or first-party evidence upgraded to independent.

Any unresolved CRITICAL answer blocks advancement; cohort averages cannot cancel it. Judge ordinary results by correctness, material completeness, traceability, comparison integrity and explicit missingness. Evaluate perceived projection usability qualitatively. Human reading time, cognitive effort and source-only confidence are excluded from controlled advancement evidence because the baseline is model-assisted; projection-session time or effort, if recorded, is descriptive only. No numeric percentage alone determines advancement and Phase 6's four-of-six threshold is not reused. After review, the product owner may choose `ADVANCE`, `ADVANCE_WITH_BOUNDED_FIX`, `REPEAT_AFTER_PRODUCT_FIX`, or `DEFER`, with recorded reasons grounded in correctness, material completeness, traceability, missingness, comparability and critical-error status.
