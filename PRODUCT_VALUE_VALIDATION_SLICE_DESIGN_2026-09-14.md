## Product hypothesis and product proposition

**Hypothesis:** For this eight-subject cohort and the specifically governed fields available after adjudication, an analyst can answer bounded Inspect and Compare questions more quickly and with equal or better material correctness and traceability from a private integrated projection than from rereading the exact retained source universe. The projection must surface gaps and `not_comparable` reasons without claiming that unreviewed or silent sources establish absence.

This is **bounded integrated multi-section product-value validation**, not broad North Star validation. It tests the integration of selected identity/purpose/activity, commitment/implementation, and outcomes/evaluation material, plus explicit evidence coverage. It does not test complete charity profiles or service capacity. It makes no charity ranking, quality score, impact score, endorsement, or investment recommendation.

### Verify — common test for every material answer

Trace answer → experiment-only governed item or explicit governed coverage state → legal/program/service scope → stable evidence locator → exact retained representation ID/hash → source carrier role → epistemic status → human adjudication ID/disposition → named reviewed-evidence universe → coverage/missingness state and period. Apply this equally to positive propositions and explicit missingness. Missing any link yields `UNVERIFIED`, a product failure for that answer. Source silence, not found in reviewed sources, not processed, unavailable, not acquired, processing failure, unknown and not applicable remain distinct states; none is a substitute for another.

## Semantic and scope rules

- **Outcomes:** activity is what was done; output/reach is a delivered count/product; outcome is an observed change with a stated measure/population/time; contribution is an attributed role; causation requires causal evidence and cannot be inferred from source attribution. Do not upgrade among these.
- **Commitments:** stated aspiration, policy, voluntary pledge, and legal/contractual commitment are distinct from evidence of implementation. First-party implementation claims are not independent verification, observed practice, compliance, or outcome.
- **Capacity/access:** service description or existence does not establish present availability, spare capacity, access eligibility, wait time, or service reach. Unknown remains unknown unless dated, scoped evidence supports more.
- **Scopes:** do not propagate an organisation-wide statement to a program/service or vice versa. RFDS Queensland Section is not the national RFDS. A program or service requires its own scope identity and evidence.
- **Evidence roles:** source fact, first-party claim, independent observation, model candidate, human adjudication, and governed proposition are separate. Model output is never itself a human decision.
- **Evidence universe:** positive and negative states are bounded by an enumerated finite reviewed universe. Silence in one representation does not establish absence elsewhere.

## Human-review protocol

1. **Source-only arm first:** for a given subject/task, the reviewer receives only the exact retained reviewed evidence universe and frozen task text. They submit and lock their answer before seeing that task’s governed projection. No raw candidate or model diagnostic is shown.
2. **Projection arm second:** the reviewer receives only the governed projection and the trace material needed for Verify. Record answer, confidence, elapsed time, cognitive effort, trace success, uncertainty visibility and perceived usefulness.
3. **No retroactive rewrite:** the source-only record is immutable after projection exposure. Where multiple reviewers are available, counterbalance task order across subjects/reviewers while preserving within-task source-only-before-projection order.
4. **Role labels:** record separately the human source-only analyst, independent human proposition adjudicator, human projection analyst, model-assisted reviewer, and model-only diagnostic. Model-only judgments never count as human evaluation. Prefer separate adjudicator and analyst; if staffing requires one independent human to do both, document the dual role and do not represent the review as blinded to candidate content.
5. Review every material answer and every explicit not-found/missingness state. Capture disagreements and rationale; do not resolve disagreement by majority or model vote without explicit human decision.

## Product-value rubric and decision frame

Score each material answer ordinally: **PASS** = correct, materially complete for the reviewed universe, traceable and qualified; **PASS_WITH_MINOR** = substantively correct and traceable with a non-material wording/omission weakness; **FAIL** = materially incomplete, misleading, untraceable, wrongly scoped or less useful than required without a critical epistemic upgrade; **CRITICAL** = untraceable or materially overstated claim, including unsupported outcome/causation, scope collapse, false evidence-of-absence, false comparability, or first-party evidence upgraded to independent.

Any unresolved CRITICAL answer blocks advancement; cohort averages cannot cancel it. Judge ordinary results together with analyst effort/time, completeness, comparison integrity and explicit missingness. No numeric percentage alone determines advancement and Phase 6's four-of-six threshold is not reused. After review, the product owner may choose `ADVANCE`, `ADVANCE_WITH_BOUNDED_FIX`, `REPEAT_AFTER_PRODUCT_FIX`, or `DEFER`, with recorded reasons grounded in correctness, material completeness, traceability, missingness, comparability, effort reduction and critical-error status.
