# Phase 5 North Star v0.2 retained-evidence completion review

**Status:** Tranche B retained-evidence review; no execution authority.
**Date:** 15 September 2026. **Active projection:** `north-star-v0.2`.

## Purpose and method

This review asks what already retained and adjudicated evidence establishes for the active North Star v0.2 contract. It is a review of records and contracts, not a new semantic pass. No provider call, source acquisition, candidate generation, promotion, runtime operation or public-v0.5 change was made.

The unit of assessment is a meaningful v0.2 requirement cluster, rather than a numeric mapping from v0.1. A cluster is `SUPPORTED` only where retained evidence and the current representation together demonstrate its bounded meaning. `PARTIAL` means some bounded evidence or reusable representation exists but a material distinction has not yet been demonstrated under v0.2. `DESIGN_ONLY` means an intended representation has not yet been empirically demonstrated. `UNTESTED` means neither retained proof nor a sufficiently applicable bounded review was found. `KNOWABILITY_ONLY` means a correctly scoped coverage state can answer the bounded question without positive content, but it does not establish substantive absence.

Section dispositions describe the whole active section, not every cluster. They are deliberately not production, scale, or Top-100 readiness claims.

## Cross-cutting families and knowability

Memberships, schemes, registrations and accreditations may project to identity, ethos, capability, relationship or classification only when the source states the relevant role, subject, period and meaning. They must not automatically become an accreditation, capability, affiliation, compliance finding or classification. Public positions, commitments and implementation are separate: policy text is a first-party commitment; self-reported action is implementation evidence; independent verification requires independently processed evidence.

For every remaining review, a missingness answer must identify the finite reviewed evidence universe, subject/scope and basis. `source_silent` says the reviewed source does not state it; `source_unavailable`, `not_acquired`, `processing_failed` and `not_processed` identify different operational limits. None establishes substantive absence, unavailability, a clean conduct record or non-implementation. These are `KNOWABILITY_ONLY` outcomes where they answer the bounded analyst question honestly.
