# CharityGraph Brand and Reuse Policy

**Status:** Canonical cross-repository policy  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-08-24

## Purpose

This policy is the single authority for CharityGraph identity, official-release status and downstream reuse. It does not create an organisation, certification scheme, legal ownership structure or registered-trade-mark claim.

## Official identity and locations

CharityGraph is the current project identity. The official repositories are:

- [CharityGraph Builder](https://github.com/gregorycwhill/charitygraph)
- [CharityGraph Data](https://github.com/gregorycwhill/charitygraph-data)
- [CharityGraph Viewer](https://github.com/gregorycwhill/charitygraph-viewer)

The canonical Viewer is published at [gregorycwhill.github.io/charitygraph-viewer](https://gregorycwhill.github.io/charitygraph-viewer/).

An **official CharityGraph release** is an immutable release published through the canonical CharityGraph Data repository and selected through the project's declared release mechanism. A repository snapshot, fork or derivative is not an official release merely because it uses CharityGraph material.

- Viewer code is available under the [MIT License](https://github.com/gregorycwhill/charitygraph-viewer/blob/main/LICENSE).
- CharityGraph-originated Data contributions are available under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), including contributions first published under the former project name.

Commercial reuse is permitted under these licences. Where the Data licence applies, reuse must provide appropriate attribution, such as “uses CharityGraph data”, identify modifications where applicable and link to the relevant release or repository. The [upstream-rights caveat](LICENSE) remains part of the Data licence guidance: third-party source material may carry separate terms and is not relicensed by CharityGraph.

## Independence and downstream products

A fork, adaptation, recommendation product or commercial service must not imply that it is CharityGraph, an official CharityGraph release, endorsed by CharityGraph or governed by CharityGraph's editorial policy. Downstream publishers may create rankings, recommendations and other opinionated views under their own methodology and branding; those outputs are not CharityGraph judgements.

`CharityGraph-compatible` may be used only as a factual compatibility description that identifies the applicable contract and version. It does not imply certification, endorsement or official status.

Modified Data must be identified as modified in accordance with CC BY 4.0. Historical CauseBase names and identifiers preserve provenance and compatibility; they do not confer current CharityGraph brand or official-release status.

CharityGraph does not currently claim to be a registered trade mark. Any future trademark evaluation is deferred and recorded in the roadmap; it is not a current requirement for reuse or release.
