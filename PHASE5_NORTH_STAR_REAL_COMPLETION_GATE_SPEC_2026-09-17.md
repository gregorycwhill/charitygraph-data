# Phase 5 North Star real completion-gate specification

**Status:** proposed gate specification on the unmerged specialist campaign branch. It is a readiness contract, not a Phase 5 completion declaration, production approval, Top-100 authorisation, or source-acquisition authorisation.

## Gate rule

Phase 5 North Star Completion may be declared only when all 20 active `north-star-v0.2` sections compile and every section has either governed, traceable, substantive support or explicit honest missingness/knowability. A section with honest missingness is complete for this bounded gate when the missingness itself is correctly represented; it is not empirically complete.

## Mechanical criteria

1. Every active v0.2 section compiles from its governed observations, relationships, CardEvidence and coverage inputs.
2. Every positive card proposition has substantive content appropriate to its predicate; role, predicate, provenance, source identifier, time, and lifecycle metadata alone are insufficient.
3. Every positive proposition has a bound subject, lowest supported scope, `about_subject_ids`, evidence locator, source record, lineage, explicit observation/reporting/effective time where asserted, predicate, payload, outcome and method.
4. Source silence, unavailable material, not acquired, not attempted, not processed, processing failure, not reviewed, withheld, stale and unknown remain distinct. They do not become asserted none or observed absence.
5. No cross-subject, scope, organisation/program, division/organisation, parent/child, or endpoint propagation creates a claim.
6. No cross-section propagation creates a claim. Activities, classifications, finance, funding, fundraising, relationships, ethos, conduct, outcomes and discovery retain their own authorised owners.
7. Epistemic basis survives projection. `source_fact`, `source_interpretation`, `governed_event`, deterministic calculation and `derived_signal` retain their controlled meaning.
8. Effective classifications retain taxonomy, version, concept, scope, method, status and lineage. Candidate, rejected, abstained and superseded entries remain traceable audit history and cannot become effective assignments.
9. Relationships remain directed, endpoint-governed, role-typed, scoped and time-qualified. An unresolved endpoint or shared domain cannot create an edge.
10. Historical v0.1 projection remains isolated from active v0.2 work.
11. No unresolved representation defect may permit a material false positive, false absence, false current-state claim, or false effective classification.

## Not required for this bounded gate

The gate does not require every section or predicate to have a positive retained example; complete data for every charity; live availability or capacity; a production freshness SLA; Top-100 scale; broad relationship-role prevalence; universal assurance; impact evidence; a dependency score; a search-quality benchmark; a correction/dispute occurrence; or zero empirical missingness.

Production freshness, correction/dispute breadth, relationship breadth, discovery quality and scale evaluation remain later policy, empirical or production concerns unless a proposed card claim asserts a current state without the required evidence and qualification.

## Required evidence for a real decision

A real gate decision must attach the all-section matrix, false-absence audit, cross-section leakage audit, traceability audit, immutable v0.5 verification, validation results, and a list of any remaining representation defects. The decision must name the fixed cohort and distinguish representation, empirical, policy and scale residuals.
