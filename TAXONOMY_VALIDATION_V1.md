# Taxonomy validation v1

**Status:** PREPARE evidence assembled; no taxonomy decision or mutation.

The new pass is taxonomy-blind before comparison: it begins with the retained
web evidence's source text, proposed domain and provenance, not ACNC labels,
existing CharityGraph classifications, organisation names or historical proposal
language. Its purpose is to ask whether the activity, beneficiary, geography,
program and participation distinctions survive real heterogeneous evidence.

## What the evidence can and cannot say

The 102-candidate pilot is narrow and noisy: 89 of 102 candidates are from
homepages and all are stable-page snapshots. It supplies useful boundary
examples (especially activity/participation, beneficiaries and geography), but
does not independently cover opportunities, self-description, fundraising or
identity. It cannot justify taxonomy expansion by itself.

The historical 120-card review recorded eight unapproved pressure points (four
HIGH, three MEDIUM, one WATCH), but its individual private proposal identities
are not part of the public project record. They must be supplied to the human
reviewer alongside the private packet; no one should pretend that aggregate
counts reproduce a particular historic pressure. Until that comparison,
every historical pressure is `CHANGES / comparison pending`, not silently
carried forward, approved or dismissed.

## Compact decision questions

1. Do the repeated web cases show a useful activity/participation boundary, or
   merely navigation/action wording that should stay outside taxonomy?
2. Is beneficiary language reliably about served groups rather than audiences,
   supporters and anecdotes?
3. Does geography evidence identify activity location rather than a contact,
   venue or broad rhetorical place reference?
4. Are named programs repeatable entities, or should many remain activity
   evidence pending stronger program identity rules?
5. For each recovered historical pressure: `PERSISTS`, `WEAKENS`,
   `DISAPPEARS`, `CHANGES` or `NEW`, with a boundary and exclusion rationale.

The governed workflow remains `PREPARE → optional MODEL REVIEW → HUMAN
DECISION → VALIDATE`. It preserves taxonomy pluralism and must not optimise
CharityGraph to mimic ACNC. A future accepted decision requires a versioned
taxonomy candidate and non-mutating validation before any reclassification or
release decision.
