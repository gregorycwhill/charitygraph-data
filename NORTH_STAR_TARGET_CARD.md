**Status:** Planning scope anchor derived from canonical product authorities
**Purpose:** Prevent local implementation work from silently narrowing CharityGraph's product scope.
**Authority:** Derived from the canonical CharityGraph product documentation. This file does **not** override `PRODUCT.md`, `PRINCIPLES.md`, `PUBLIC_COMMITMENTS.md`, `EXPERIENCES.md`, `INTEGRATED_PRODUCT_AND_DATA_MODEL.md`, `DOMAIN_PROFILE_INDEX.md`, or approved domain authorities.

## How to use this file

Periodically upload this file into the active CharityGraph conversation and ask:

> **What are we working on now, where does it fit in this North Star, what parts of the North Star remain untouched, and are there opportunities to reuse the same evidence/LLM work across multiple domains?**

The answer should explicitly distinguish:

1. the current implementation or experiment;
2. the North Star sections it directly advances;
3. adjacent sections that can reuse the same evidence or model work;
4. important product domains currently untouched;
5. whether the work is accidentally turning one slice into the product;
6. whether one evidence packet could support multiple independent semantic tasks;
7. whether a proposed task should be deterministic, model-assisted, or human-governed;
8. whether scale-up is premature relative to cross-domain learning.

### Anti-drift rule

> **Program/service discovery is one section of CharityGraph, not CharityGraph itself.**

Builder progress should be discussed in terms of which North Star sections and cross-domain relationships it can populate reliably—not how far one semantic slice has been perfected.

### Cross-domain LLM rule

> **When a costly evidence packet is already being acquired or sent to a model, actively check whether compatible independent observations for other North Star domains can be extracted or assessed from the same material without corrupting domain semantics or provenance.**

This does **not** mean one giant schema or one undifferentiated model judgment. Separate logical outputs, validation, scope, evidence and provenance should remain independently governed even when work is bundled economically.

# North Star Target Card

This is a **maximal organisation projection**: what CharityGraph should ultimately be capable of showing or answering about one charity when evidence exists.

It is **not** a giant stored record and not every charity must have every field.

The underlying model remains linked subjects, observations, measurements, assertions, relationships, decisions and evidence. The card/page is a convenient projection over governed knowledge.

# Product-level guardrails

1. **One-stop shop means integrated governed discovery, not one table.**
2. **Cards are projections, not Builder's internal source of truth.**
3. **Put facts at the lowest scope supported by evidence.**
4. **Legal purpose does not prove current activity.**
5. **Partner facts do not automatically become organisation facts.**
6. **Source-reported facts and CharityGraph judgments remain distinct.**
7. **Authority is claim-specific.**
8. **Missingness is explicit and non-negative.**
9. **No universal charity quality, trust, ethos, impact or efficiency score.**
10. **Program/service discovery is only one domain.**
11. **Use taxonomies for distinctions we are ready to name and govern; embeddings may preserve distinctions we are not yet ready to name.**
12. **Use models for open-ended semantics; do not teach Python English.**
13. **Reuse evidence and model compute across domains where compatible, but keep logical outputs independently governed.**
14. **A model result is not automatically governed knowledge or publishable data.**
15. **Every published assertion retains reconstructible provenance.**
16. **Scale is a product goal, but scale should not erase semantic breadth.**
