# CharityGraph North Star Target Card vNext

**Status:** Active internal product-planning projection contract, adopted 15 September 2026. Not a public schema or a claim of production completeness.

This active projection is versioned separately from [historical North Star v0.1](NORTH_STAR_TARGET_CARD_V0_1.md). Section numbers have meaning only with their projection-contract version. Historical experiments, assignments, coverage matrices, decisions and artifacts retain their original meanings; no automatic migration or semantic equivalence is implied.

## Purpose and use

The card is a projection over governed evidence, observations, assertions, relationships, measurements, decisions and coverage?not a stored mega-record or model response. One proposition may support multiple views when each is evidence-supported. Use the lowest evidence-supported scope: organisation, program or service. Positive content is not required for every organisation.

## Active vNext section structure

1. **Identity / regulatory** ? identity, legal form and regulatory status.
2. **Purpose / cause** ? stated purpose, mandate, cause and separately assessed alignment; legal purpose is not proof of current activity.
3. **Programs / services** ? programs, projects, campaigns and services with explicit scope.
4. **Activities / SDGs** ? activities and bounded taxonomy relationships; activity is not outcome or impact.
5. **Geography / beneficiaries** ? operating geography and intended or served populations, preserving source and scope.
6. **Participation** ? membership, volunteering, participation and engagement where supported.
7. **Direct service / capacity** ? service description, eligibility, access, current availability and capacity as distinct concepts; unknown capacity is not zero capacity.
8. **Fundraising** ? fundraising methods, campaigns and resource mobilisation, distinguished from revenue and expenditure.
9. **Governance / leadership** ? governing structures, officeholders and decision roles with temporal scope.
10. **People / workforce** ? paid workforce, contractors and volunteers as distinct populations and measures.
11. **Scale / capability** ? supported measures or descriptions; do not infer capability from size alone.
12. **Relationships** ? directed, typed roles among entities; preserve operator, deliverer, funder, sponsor, partner, auspice and network distinctions.
13. **Finances** ? source-faithful financial concepts, periods, units and resource flows.
14. **Funding / dependencies** ? grants, funding dependencies and typed funding relationships; do not infer dependency from one transaction.
15. **Ethos / values** ? institutional self-description, affiliations and values, separated from CharityGraph assessment.
16. **Conduct / adverse** ? adverse matters, compliance and responses with source, status, temporal and adjudication context.
17. **Notable history** ? dated historical events and context, distinguishing documented fact from later interpretation.
18. **Evaluation / outcomes** ? evaluation designs and findings; keep activity, output, outcome, impact, contribution and causation distinct.
19. **Classification / search / AI discovery** ? source-reported classifications separate from CharityGraph-assessed assignments and discovery lenses.
20. **Evidence / coverage / freshness / corrections** ? provenance and locators, coverage/missingness, review dates, freshness, corrections and supersession.

## Epistemic and projection rules

- A first-party statement is a source-reported claim, not an independent fact. Preserve attribution, time and scope.
- Distinguish source fact, first-party claim, model-generated candidate/inference and human decision. Model output is not governed knowledge without applicable review and promotion.
- A policy or commitment does not establish implementation. A service description does not establish current availability or capacity.
- Missingness is not absence. Preserve existing distinctions such as evidence present, not found in reviewed sources, source silent, not processed, source unavailable, not acquired, processing failed, unknown and not applicable. Do not collapse these to one null or negative.
- Relationships are directed and typed. Shared branding, source domains or co-location do not establish a relationship.
- Source-reported classification remains distinct from CharityGraph-assessed classification. All claims and assessments remain traceable to evidence locators.

## Historical compatibility and completion framing

The v0.1 card remains available unchanged at [NORTH_STAR_TARGET_CARD_V0_1.md](NORTH_STAR_TARGET_CARD_V0_1.md). For example, v0.1 section 7 is fundraising/resource mobilisation; vNext section 7 is direct service/capacity. Reuse underlying governed observations only through explicit versioned assignments; do not rewrite historical identities or artifacts.

Phase 5 North Star Completion seeks bounded evidence that each active section has governed support or a tested, honest explanation of why knowledge is unavailable, unprocessed, source-silent, not applicable or unknown. It does not require positive facts everywhere and does not establish production completeness. Top-100 scale requires a separate completion gate and product-owner decision.
